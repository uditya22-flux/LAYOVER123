## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an exuberant, tactile world that fuses claymorphism with refined neo-neumorphic illumination. Tailored for adventurous explorers and culture seekers seeking unconventional experiences, the aesthetic transforms everyday mobile interactions into a vibrant, pillowy toybox.

### Visual Aesthetic & Mood
- **Tactile Soft Neo-Neumorphism**: Surfaces are squishy, elevated, and volumetric. They feature glossy specular highlights along top boundaries and deep, diffused, atmospheric drop shadows that suggest weight and playful gravity.
- **Dreamy Candy Twilight**: A vibrant sky palette dominated by twilight periwinkle, sunset coral-pink, punchy fuchsia, golden honey, and minty pasture greens.
- **Sensory Delight**: Elements invite touch through inflated pill geometries, multi-layer card decks, juicy push states, and candy-coated gradients.
- **Gamified Optimism**: Playful micro-rewards, XP chips, streak badges, and whimsical typography keep the emotional tone cheerful, curious, and welcoming.

## Colors

The palette draws its energy from an iridescent sunset over lush hills, anchored by a deep periwinkle and ignited by hyper-saturated coral and candy accents.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#6366F1`)**: Twilight Periwinkle. Used for active navigation tabs, foundational brand iconography, progress tracks, and primary structural accents.
- **Secondary (`#F43F5E`)**: Punchy Bubblegum Coral. Drives high-conversion primary actions, main quest call-to-actions, and playful "Wild" rating indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Spring Mint. Designates exploration markers, success celebrations, completed missions, and positive environmental indicators.
- **Neutral (`#1E1B4B`)**: Midnight Indigo. Anchors headings and high-contrast labels without the harshness of pure black, maintaining harmony with pastel underlays.

### Auxiliary Accent Tones
- **Electric Candy Fuchsia (`#EC4899`)**: Complements the secondary coral for multi-stop linear gradient fills.
- **Golden Sunray (`#FBBF24`)**: XP rewards, streak indicators, and star badges.
- **Atmospheric Sky Mist (`#E0E7FF` to `#FCE7F3`)**: Background canvas gradient, running from soft morning azure to twilight peach.
- **Porcelain Pill Surface (`#FFFFFF` at 92%–100% opacity)**: The base container fill for readable cards, stacked decks, and floating control bars.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes bold, round, geometric displays with highly legible humanist body text.

### Font Hierarchy
- **Headline & Labels (`Outfit`)**: Delivers chunky, buoyant, expressive titling. Used in weights `700` and `800` for banner headings, XP callouts, interactive buttons, and stat values.
- **Body & Captions (`Be Vietnam Pro`)**: Provides crisp legibility on compact mobile screens. Used across review summaries, mission details, and instructional copy.

### Text Styling Notes
- Prominent banner titles (e.g., Quest Headers, Celebration Banners) leverage full uppercase typesetting with subtle inner dropshadows (`0 1px 2px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.25)`) to heighten the 3D toy feel.

## Layout & Spacing

Designed specifically around the handheld portrait frame (target 393 × 852 pt), the layout model enforces a breathable, vertical-flow hierarchy that accommodates chunky interactive elements.

### Structural Rhythm
- **Outer Canvas Margins**: Set to `1.25rem` (20px) on standard mobile viewports, tightening to `1rem` on compact devices to preserve touch targets.
- **Component Padding Scale**: Internal card paddings default to `space-lg` (16px), giving layered card headers and stacked card decks sufficient boundary clearance.
- **Interaction Targets**: All touch targets (action pills, mood selectors, thumbnail previews) strictly maintain a minimum tap height of 48px to preserve fluid ergonomics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on flat outlines or stark dropshadows. Depth is manifested through volumetric physical simulation: pneumatic drop shadows, multi-tiered stacking, and glazed ambient highlights.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Recessed / Sunken (Inputs, Progress Well)**
   - Fill: Inner cavity tint (`rgba(224, 231, 255, 0.45)`).
   - Shadow: `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.12)`, `inset 0 -1px 2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.

2. **Level 1: Cloud Surface (Resting Tiles & Mood Selectors)**
   - Fill: `#FFFFFF` or subtle translucent candy tint.
   - Surface Border: 1.5px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`.
   - Shadow: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`, `0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.

3. **Level 2: Pillowy Hero Cards & Stacked Decks**
   - Fill: `#FFFFFF`.
   - Surface Border: 2px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1)`.
   - Stack Offsets: Sub-cards rotate -2deg / +2deg with offset top margins, creating a tactile physical deck.
   - Shadow: `0 16px 28px -6px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.18)`, `0 4px 10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.

4. **Level 3: Squishy Action Buttons & Floating Nav**
   - Fill: Multi-stop saturated gradient (`#F43F5E` to `#FB7185`).
   - Glaze Effect: `inset 0 2px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`, `inset 0 -3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)`.
   - Shadow: `0 10px 22px -3px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.42)`, `0 3px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular is defined by organic, hyper-soft curves and exaggerated pill forms.

### Corner Radius Implementation
- **Base Level (`roundedness: 2`)**: Base elements carry an 8px radius.
- **Card Enclosures & Modules (`rounded-2xl` / 24px–28px)**: Large rectangular panels (curious spot cards, vibe selector blocks) use ultra-smooth squircle-like corners.
- **Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: All primary call-to-actions, status chips, bottom navigation docks, and streak trackers use fully rounded pill profiles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Candy Pill)**: Full pill-shaped geometry (`rounded-full`). Filled with vibrant gradient (`#F43F5E` to `#EC4899`). Features top inner specular highlight (`inset 0 2px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`) and bottom compression bevel (`inset 0 -3px 0 rgba(190, 18, 60, 0.35)`). Active pressed state collapses the bottom bevel (`translateY(2px)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill**: Frosted translucent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` backdrop blur 12px) with crisp 1.5px white border and soft purple ambient drop shadow.

### Cards & Stacked Decks
- **Tactile Quest Cards**: Multi-layer stacked presentation. The top card carries the destination hero photography with rounded-xl internal media corners, an absolute-positioned top-right rating pill, and bottom action descriptors. Under-cards peek through with alternating candy borders (yellow, cyan, coral).
- **Vibe Category Cards**: Square-aspect pillowy boxes featuring 3D clay emojis (Tornado, Mushroom, Clown, Radio). Background uses soft tint matching the emoji vibe with a 2px inner white sheen.

### Badges & XP Chips
- **Rating Badges**: Pill-shaped coral tag (`#F43F5E`) with crisp white typography and a solid yellow star icon (`★ 1.2`).
- **Streak & XP Counter**: Golden gradient capsule (`#FBBF24` to `#F59E0B`) with micro fire or sun badges, set with heavy Outfit bold numbering.

### Progress Bars & Sliders
- **Candy Progress Bar**: Inset sunken track filled with periwinkle wash, housing a bouncy rounded pill fill with animated diagonal candy-cane or solid bright mint fill.
- **Radar Range Slider**: Thick recessed gutter with an oversized, squishy spherical coral thumb knob that expands slightly on drag.

### Navigation Dock
- **Floating Island**: Suspended pill dock with backdrop blur (20px), white translucent glaze (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), containing icon-label duos where the active item pops into an illuminated coral or periwinkle micro-pill.
